Privacy Policy
Last Updated: October 26, 2025

TL;DR - The Short Version

Pack Saver does not collect ANY data about you. Everything happens locally in your browser. We don't track you, sell your data, or even know you're using the extension. Your privacy is 100% protected.

Introduction

Pack Saver ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Pack Saver handles (or rather, doesn't handle) your data when you use our Chrome browser extension.

Data Collection

We collect ZERO data.

Pack Saver does not collect, store, transmit, or share any information about:

  • Your browsing history
  • Shopping activity or purchases
  • Product searches or views
  • Personal information
  • Location data
  • Usage statistics
  • Any other identifiable information

How Pack Saver Works

Pack Saver operates entirely within your browser:

  • Local Processing: All price calculations happen on your device
  • No External Connections: The extension doesn't communicate with any external servers
  • No Analytics: We don't use Google Analytics or any other tracking service
  • No User Accounts: No sign-up, no login, no user profiles

Data Storage

The ONLY data stored by Pack Saver is:

  • Extension On/Off State: A simple preference to remember if you've disabled the extension on specific sites
  • Location: Stored locally on your device using Chrome's storage API
  • Transmission: This data never leaves your computer

Permissions Explained

Pack Saver requests the following permissions, and here's exactly why:

  • activeTab: To read product information on the shopping page you're viewing (only when active)
  • scripting: To display price badges and the overlay on product pages
  • storage: To save your on/off preference locally

These permissions are used ONLY for the extension's core functionality and nothing else.

Third-Party Services

Pack Saver does not use any third-party services.

We don't integrate with:

  • Analytics platforms
  • Advertising networks
  • Data brokers
  • Tracking services
  • External APIs

Cookies

Pack Saver does not use cookies or any other tracking technologies.

Data Sharing

Since we don't collect any data, we have nothing to share. We will never:

  • Sell your data
  • Share your data with advertisers
  • Provide your data to third parties
  • Use your data for marketing

Children's Privacy

Pack Saver is safe for users of all ages. Since we collect no data whatsoever, there are no special privacy concerns for children.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change how Pack Saver handles data (though we have no plans to), we will:

  • Update this privacy policy
  • Update the "Last Updated" date
  • Notify users through the Chrome Web Store update notes

Your Rights

Under GDPR and other privacy laws, you have certain rights regarding your data. Since Pack Saver doesn't collect any data, these rights don't really apply - but just so you know:

  • Right to Access: There's no data to access
  • Right to Deletion: There's no data to delete
  • Right to Portability: There's no data to export
  • Right to Object: There's no processing to object to

The only "data" is your local on/off preference, which you can reset by reinstalling the extension.

Open Source

Pack Saver's code is open source, which means:

  • Anyone can review the code to verify our privacy claims
  • Security researchers can audit the extension
  • You can see exactly what the extension does

Pack Saver is built with privacy as the top priority. We believe you should be able to shop online and compare prices without being tracked, profiled, or having your data sold. That's why we designed Pack Saver to work entirely on your device with zero data collection.
